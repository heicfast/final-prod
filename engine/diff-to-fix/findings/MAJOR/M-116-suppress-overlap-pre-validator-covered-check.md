# MAJOR M-116 — the suppress_overlap trial loop runs the FULL cap-sized validator walk for every mid-stream sync BEFORE the covered check discards the result: contiguous 1-byte-sync streams (TS/M2TS/MPEG-audio) pay a quadratic re-walk that makes the carve stage not finish — discovered during S-054's fixture verification

**Severity:** MAJOR (performance — the scan stage hangs on mainstream contiguous streams; the walk's output is discarded, so no results change)
**Module:** carve (the grammar layer's trial loop)
**Parameters affected:** P47 (throughput — the per-candidate validator cost), A27-adjacent (the walked extent), C20-adjacent (the cap that bounds the wasted walk)
**Discovered:** session 40, during S-054's fail-before/behavior-test construction (§8 of the session mandate — announced in the landing commit, never silently fixed)

**Core references (the defect, read first-hand at the fix):** `src/core/carve/cr_carve.cpp` `scan_one_window` — the per-byte trial loop called `sig.validator(&wdev, cand_abs, sig.max_size)` (the full packet walk, bounded by the row's cap) BEFORE consulting `emitted_at` and `ctx.overlap_covered`; both checks `continue`d/duplicated without ever using the walk's result. For a contiguous suppress_overlap stream (TS/M2TS: the 1-byte 0x47 magic matches at EVERY 188-byte packet boundary) the trial loop therefore re-walked up to the row's FULL cap for every mid-stream sync after the first emission. **Executed proof:** the S-054 behavior fixture (a 220 MB contiguous sync-valid TS stream) ran >600 s without completing pre-fix (the session's own timeout, twice); post-fix the identical fixture completes in 11.3 s, of which the scan's inherent per-byte window cost is the remainder (the walks are gone — ~1.06M mid-stream syncs now resolve at the covered check).

**The og side (honest scope):** the corpus does not decode the og's block-scan position-advance micro-behavior (rawsearch-carving--deep-dive.md documents the analyser contract and the confidence RANKING of overlapping candidates, N-070's decode — "Header+tail+size-gate confidence combination ranks overlapping candidates"); no og-divergence claim is made. This finding is a core-internal ordering defect: the covered candidates' terminal state (duplicates++ + continue) was already the design's outcome — the walk work preceding it was pure discarded cost.

**Impact:** every real-world contiguous TS/M2TS/MPEG-audio stream larger than a few MB (camcorder .m2ts captures, broadcast .ts recordings — contiguous by nature) multiplied the carve stage's walk cost by the packet count: a 200 MB .ts = ~1.06M re-walks × up to 200 MB each. The stage's completion time stops being bounded by device size. No results divergence (the walk outputs were discarded) — hence MAJOR, not SEVERE.
